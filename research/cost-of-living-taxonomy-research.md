### Life Insurance Risk Classes
1. **Preferred Plus / Preferred Select** — Excellent health, no family history of major diseases, ideal BMI, no tobacco

4. **Standard** — Average risk, baseline pricing

# 6. Unified Taxonomy: All Dimensions Affecting Human Life

Based on the above research, here is a comprehensive taxonomy of "things in the world that affect human life":

## Dimension 1: CONSUMPTION (What you buy)
*Covered by: COICOP, CPI baskets*
1. Food and nutrition
2. Beverages (alcoholic and non-alcoholic)
3. Tobacco and narcotics
4. Clothing and footwear
5. Housing (rent, utilities, maintenance)
6. Household goods and equipment
7. Health products and services
8. Transport (vehicles, fuel, services)
9. Communication and information technology
10. Recreation, sport and culture
11. Education
12. Restaurants and hospitality
13. Insurance and financial services
14. Personal care and miscellaneous

## Dimension 2: INCOME & EMPLOYMENT (How you earn)
*Covered by: ISCO-08, labour force surveys*
1. Occupation type and skill level
2. Employment status (employed, self-employed, unemployed, retired)
3. Industry sector
4. Income level and stability
5. Benefits and social protection
6. Career progression and mobility

## Dimension 3: ASSETS & WEALTH (What you own)
*Covered by: SNA, banking classifications*
1. Real estate and land
2. Financial instruments (stocks, bonds, funds, deposits)
3. Business ownership
4. Physical assets (vehicles, gold, collectibles)
5. Digital assets
6. Intellectual property
7. Pension and retirement assets

## Dimension 4: LIABILITIES & DEBT (What you owe)
*Covered by: Credit bureaus, banking*
1. Secured debt (mortgage, auto loans, collateralized loans)
2. Unsecured debt (personal loans, credit cards, student loans)
3. Contingent liabilities (guarantees, litigation)
4. Tax obligations

## Dimension 5: HEALTH & WELLBEING (Your physical and mental state)
*Covered by: ICD-11, disability classifications*
1. Infectious and parasitic diseases
2. Chronic conditions (cardiovascular, respiratory, metabolic)
3. Mental health and neurodevelopmental conditions
4. Musculoskeletal conditions
5. Sensory impairments (vision, hearing)
6. Reproductive and sexual health
7. Injuries and external causes
8. Disability status
9. Nutritional status

## Dimension 6: ENVIRONMENTAL EXPOSURE (Where you live)
*Covered by: OECD hazard framework, environmental classifications*
1. Climate hazards (heat, cold, precipitation, drought, storms)
2. Geophysical hazards (earthquakes, volcanoes, landslides)
3. Air quality (outdoor and indoor pollution)
4. Water quality
5. Noise and light pollution
6. Biodiversity and ecosystem health
7. Urban vs. rural setting
8. Infrastructure quality (roads, water supply, sanitation, electricity)

## Dimension 7: LEGAL & REGULATORY EXPOSURE
1. Tax regime
2. Employment regulations
3. Property rights
4. Family law (marriage, inheritance, custody)
5. Criminal justice exposure
6. Consumer protection
7. Immigration status
8. Data privacy
9. Financial regulation
10. Environmental compliance
11. Social security/welfare eligibility

## Dimension 8: SOCIAL & DEMOGRAPHIC FACTORS
1. Age and life stage
2. Gender
3. Family composition (household size, dependents, marital status)
4. Education level
5. Social networks and community
6. Caste/ethnicity/race (in relevant contexts)
7. Religion
8. Language
9. Migration/displacement status
10. Digital literacy and access

## Dimension 9: PRODUCTION & SUPPLY (What is made — affects prices of everything)
*Covered by: WPI, PPI, commodity indices*
1. Agricultural commodities (food, fibres, plantation crops)
2. Minerals and metals
3. Energy (crude oil, natural gas, coal, electricity)
4. Manufactured goods (22 NIC sub-groups in India's WPI)
5. Services production
6. Technology and innovation
7. Global supply chains

## Dimension 10: MACROECONOMIC ENVIRONMENT
1. Inflation (CPI, WPI, core inflation)
2. Interest rates (repo rate, lending rates, deposit rates)
3. Exchange rates
4. GDP growth
5. Government fiscal policy (spending, taxation, subsidies)
6. Monetary policy
7. Trade policy (tariffs, quotas, FTAs)
8. Labour market conditions (unemployment rate, labour force participation)
